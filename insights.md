# Key insights: Superstore sales analysis (2011-2014)

**1. Profit per sale dropped in 2014, and two regions caused it.**
Sales grew 52% from 2011 to 2014, but the margin fell from 13.4% to 12.7% in 2014. Central fell from 13.5% to 5.1% and South from 18.9% to 7.2%.
**Action:** review Central and South product lines that lost money in 2014.

**2. Big discounts lose money.**
Orders with 30% or more off lost about $135K in total, while total profit was $286K.
**Action:** cap discounts at 20% unless a manager approves.

**3. Central gives too many big discounts.**
28% of Central orders had a discount of 30% or more, against 4% in the West. Central's margin is 7.9%, and the West's is 14.9%.
**Action:** apply the West's discount rules to Central.

**4. Some products lose money.**
Tables lost $17.7K, Bookcases lost $3.5K, and Supplies lost $1.2K. Machines has $189K of sales but only a 1.8% margin.
**Action:** raise prices or reduce discounts on Tables and Bookcases.

**5. A few products matter a lot.**
One Canon copier earns $25K, about 9% of all profit. Some 3D printers lose $4K to $9K each.
**Action:** keep the top products in stock and review the worst ones.
